**Figure N | Isobutanol TRY scouts visit designs above the 15.8 % ethanol-only plateau of this work's uninformed profitability campaign but return low-IRR designs. Seeded with the scouts' trials, profitability search co-produces above that plateau on trial 2, beats every seed by trial 13 and reaches 27.3 %.** Eight Gaussian-process campaigns searched one 12-dimensional strain-design and feeding space. Two maximized profitability (profitability index, shown as internal rate of return, IRR): **uninformed** (cyan, 2,000 trials) and **TRY-informed** (teal, 1,000 trials, started from 1,000 seeds). The seeds were 327 of the 330 scout trials at least as profitable as the starting strain (2 convergence-quarantined and one duplicate dropped) and 673 space-filling ones. Six TRY campaigns (the scouts) each maximized one titer, rate or yield of ethanol (ethanol TRY, amber) or isobutanol (isobutanol TRY, violet). The TRY-informed campaign's 1,000 trials do not count the scouts' 11,968 simulations. Trial: simulated index, 1-based (preloaded seeds excluded); only completed trials are shown. Dashed cyan: uninformed plateau, 15.8 % (tinted above); dotted grey: starting strain, 12.6 %; dotted violet: best seed, 22.9 %; loss: IRR < 0 or none. Making isobutanol: ≥ 5 g·L⁻¹; co-production: ≥ 5 g·L⁻¹ of each alcohol.

**(a)** Best IRR so far. The uninformed campaign plateaued at 15.8 % (ethanol only) from trial 104 to 2,000 (cyan strip: its space-filling start-up; the TRY-informed campaign had none). Left: the seeds by scout family; all 112 above 15.8 % are from isobutanol TRY campaigns, four of them co-producers (teal rings; best 21.1 %). The TRY-informed campaign lost money on trial 1, co-produced at 19.8 % on trial 2, beat every seed on trial 13 (23.8 %) and reached 25.3 % on trial 18; every later improvement co-produced. Inset (linear trial axis): refinement from 26.2 % (trial 25) to 27.3 % (trial 913), a PI gain of 0.074, seven times the 0.0105 reproducibility margin.

**(b)** IRR vs isobutanol's share of the alcohol titer, all 13,611 designs making ≥ 1 g·L⁻¹ alcohol. Number beside the 100 % column: the 107 isobutanol-only scout designs above 15.8 % that overlap there. Open violet ○ □ △: isobutanol TRY best visits (as in c); ○ is the best seed. ◆, ★: uninformed and TRY-informed bests (★: 41 g·L⁻¹ ethanol + 40 g·L⁻¹ isobutanol); all 296 designs above 22.9 % are TRY-informed co-producers.

*Methods notes (not part of the legend).*
* Random seed 350 for all eight campaigns. Every campaign starts from scenario A and is subject to the enzyme-burden constraint; titers are per litre of water. The TRY-informed campaign's preload (1,000 trials: 592 from the isobutanol TRY and 408 from the ethanol TRY campaigns) exceeded its start-up count, so it had no space-filling start-up: its GP proposed every trial.
* Trial = CSV trial_number + 1 (TRY-informed: trial_number − 999; its best, trial 913, is trial_number 1,912). FAIL rows, excluded everywhere: uninformed 3, TRY-informed 2, ethanol TRY 6/1/3 and isobutanol TRY 14/3/5 (yield / titer / productivity).
* PI = net present value at a 15 % hurdle rate / total capital investment. The reproducibility margin is the cross-process margin of the deterministic PI objective, ΔPI 0.0105 (about 0.2 IRR points); smaller differences are not interpreted.
* The seeds were preloaded with their recorded objective values, without re-simulation; the 112 scout trials above 15.8 % are among them (108 isobutanol-only, < 5 g·L⁻¹ ethanol; 4 co-producers). The 327 were picked by PI, so the seed set is not blind to profitability.
* No compute-matched control (e.g. 12,968 random or space-filling seeds) was run. Two 4,091-design Sobol' random samples of the same space bracket it: under the campaigns' own sampling measure no design exceeded the 15 % hurdle (best 13.7 %); under a linear engineering prior 19 did (best 21.5 %, below the best seed). Seeding is shown to buy speed and reliability here, not unique access to designs above the plateau.
* (b) omits the 1,352 designs making < 1 g·L⁻¹ alcohol (none has an IRR); ethanol TRY designs lie mostly under the uninformed column. The uninformed campaign proposed 95 of its 97 isobutanol-making designs after trial 104 (5 % of its later trials). No design making < 5 g·L⁻¹ isobutanol exceeds 15.8 % (b's title). The one other isobutanol-only scout design above 15.8 % is the titer best visit (□). The outlined designs' label sits on a translucent white backing that dims, but does not hide, the designs behind it. (c) The ethanol TRY campaigns returned 8.1–12.7 %.
* (f) The replicate uninformed campaign made isobutanol in 38 % of its trials and found its best design at trial 1,603; the unseeded runs reached 25 % only at trial 104, or never. The seeded runs' best seeds were 22.9 % and 25.1 %.
* (e) Pdc and ALS→Aro10 commit carbon to ethanol and isobutanol. The translation (φ_T = 0.110) and housekeeping sectors are omitted; the four designs beyond the budget grow at ×0.84, ×0.63, ×0.30, ×0.997 of their penalty-free rate. The scouts' 11,968 simulations are by-products of the TRY campaigns.
* The best design lies at five search bounds, so higher IRR may exist outside the searched ranges.
* IRRs are at default prices under the model version used for the campaigns (starting strain 12.6 %; 12.8 % under the current model version).
