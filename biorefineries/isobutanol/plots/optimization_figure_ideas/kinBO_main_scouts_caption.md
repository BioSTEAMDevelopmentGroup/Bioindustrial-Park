In these single runs, neither objective alone returned a profitable co-producer: the unseeded profitability campaign plateaued at 15.8 % on ethanol, and the scouts returned ethanol-only or isobutanol-only designs. The seeded route used 13,000 simulations (12,000 scout + 1,000), the uninformed campaign 2,000.

*Methods notes (not part of the legend).*
* Random seed 350 for all eight campaigns. Every campaign starts from scenario A and is subject to the enzyme-burden constraint; titers are per litre of water. The TRY-informed campaign's preload (1,000 trials: 592 from the isobutanol TRY and 408 from the ethanol TRY campaigns) exceeded its start-up count, so it had no space-filling start-up: its GP proposed every trial. The seeds were preloaded with their recorded objective values, without re-simulation. The 327 are the 330 scout trials at least as profitable as the starting strain minus 2 convergence-quarantined and one duplicate; picked by PI, so the seed set is not blind to profitability. The 112 scout trials above 15.8 % are among them (108 isobutanol-only, < 5 g·L⁻¹ ethanol; 4 co-producers, best 21.1 %).
* Trial = CSV trial_number + 1 (TRY-informed: trial_number − 999; its best, trial 913, is trial_number 1,912), 1-based, preloaded seeds excluded; only completed trials are shown. FAIL rows, excluded everywhere: uninformed 3, TRY-informed 2, ethanol TRY 6/1/3 and isobutanol TRY 14/3/5 (yield / titer / productivity); simulation counts include them.
* PI = net present value at a 15 % hurdle rate / total capital investment. PI is deterministic but depends slightly on the converged state each simulation starts from (the previous trial's). The reproducibility margin, ΔPI 0.0075, is the largest PI difference between two campaigns' simulations of the same design after different trial histories (49 pairs; 0.13 IRR points at that pair); smaller differences are not interpreted.
* (a) The uninformed campaign plateaued at 15.8 % (ethanol only) from trial 104 to 2,000; the cyan strip is its space-filling start-up. The TRY-informed campaign lost money on trial 1, co-produced at 19.8 % on trial 2, held an isobutanol-only incumbent (21.6 %) from trial 3 to trial 6, and every improvement from trial 7 on co-produced; it beat every seed on trial 13 (23.8 %), reached 25.3 % on trial 18, and refined 26.2 % (trial 25) to 27.3 % (trial 913), a PI gain of 0.074, ten times the reproducibility margin. All 112 seeds above 15.8 % are from isobutanol TRY campaigns, four of them co-producers.
* (b) Shown: 13,611 designs; the 1,352 making < 1 g·L⁻¹ alcohol (none has an IRR) are omitted; ethanol TRY designs lie mostly under the uninformed column. The uninformed campaign's 97 isobutanol-making designs were all worse than its plateau (best 13.9 %) and more often lost money (64 % vs 33 %); it proposed 95 of them after trial 104 (5 % of its later trials). Their label's opaque backing hides 3 of them and 12 other designs (never the best one). The 100 % column holds 107 isobutanol-only scout designs above 15.8 % (drawn over the TRY-informed dots) and 20 TRY-informed ones; the one other isobutanol-only scout design above 15.8 % is the titer best visit (□). No design making < 5 g·L⁻¹ isobutanol exceeds 15.8 % (b's title); all 296 designs above 22.9 % are TRY-informed co-producers; ★ makes 41 g·L⁻¹ ethanol + 40 g·L⁻¹ isobutanol.

Pdc and ALS→Aro10 commit carbon to ethanol and isobutanol.
