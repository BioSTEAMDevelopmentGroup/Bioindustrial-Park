*Methods notes (not part of the legend).*
* Every campaign starts from scenario A and is subject to the enzyme-burden constraint; titers are per litre of water. The TRY-informed campaign's preload (1,000 trials) exceeded its start-up count, so it had no space-filling start-up.
* PI = net present value at a 15 % hurdle rate / total capital investment. The reproducibility margin is the cross-process margin of the deterministic PI objective, ΔPI 0.0105 (about 0.2 IRR points); smaller differences are not interpreted.
* The seeds were preloaded with their recorded objective values, without re-simulation. The scouts' 11,968 simulations are by-products of the TRY campaigns and are not counted on the trial axes.
* The best design lies at five search bounds, so higher IRR may exist outside the searched ranges.
* IRRs are at default prices under the model version used for the campaigns (starting strain 12.6 %; 12.8 % under the current model version).
