**Figure N | Process-level (TRY) campaigns scout the isobutanol designs that a profitability-only search rarely explores, and seeding the profitability search with their trials finds a profitable ethanol + isobutanol co-production strain.** Eight Gaussian-process campaigns searched the same 12-dimensional strain-design and feeding space (scenario-A start, enzyme-burden constraint, seed 350). Two maximized profitability (profitability index, PI = net present value at a 15 % hurdle rate / total capital investment; shown as internal rate of return, IRR): **uninformed** (cyan; 2,000 trials, the first 51 a space-filling design shared with the TRY campaigns) and **TRY-informed** (teal; 1,000 trials; its Gaussian process was preloaded with 1,000 trials of the six TRY campaigns). Six TRY campaigns ("scouts") each maximized one titer, rate (productivity) or yield of ethanol (amber) or isobutanol (violet) (○ yield, □ titer, △ productivity). Dashed cyan: the uninformed plateau, 15.8 % IRR; tinted: IRR above it; dotted grey: the starting strain (scenario A), 12.6 %; "loss": IRR < 0 or no IRR (an outright money-loser). Titers are per litre of water; "making isobutanol" means ≥ 5 g·L⁻¹; "co-production" means ≥ 5 g·L⁻¹ of each alcohol.

**(a)** IRR of the best design so far, which is also each campaign's incumbent, vs simulated trials. The uninformed campaign reached 15.8 % with an ethanol-only design at trial 104 and did not improve in the remaining 1,896 trials. Left strip: the 1,000 seed trials by scout family. All 112 seeds above 15.8 % come from isobutanol scouts; the best is 22.9 %. The TRY-informed campaign's first trial lost money; its second (19.8 %) already co-produced both alcohols; its 13th (23.8 %) beat every seed; it passed 25 % at trial 18 and 26.2 % at trial 25. Inset: refinement to 27.3 % at trial 913 (PI 0.73 → 0.81, seven times the cross-process reproducibility margin of ΔPI ≈ 0.01).

**(b)** IRR of every simulated design vs isobutanol's share of the alcohol titer. 13,611 designs; the 1,352 designs making < 1 g·L⁻¹ alcohol all have no IRR and are omitted. None of the 9,885 designs making < 5 g·L⁻¹ isobutanol, in any campaign, exceeds 15.8 %. The uninformed campaign's 97 isobutanol-making designs were all worse (best 13.9 %) and lost money more often (64 % vs 33 %). The isobutanol scouts' best visits (open violet markers) reached 22.9 % with pure isobutanol. All 296 designs above 22.9 % are TRY-informed co-production designs (★, 27.3 %: 40.6 g·L⁻¹ ethanol + 39.6 g·L⁻¹ isobutanol).

**(c)** Every completed trial of each campaign (strip), its highest-IRR visit (open) and the design it returned, the argmax of its own objective (filled). Right: trials above 15.8 % and the share of trials losing money (1,986–1,999 completed trials per campaign; TRY-informed 998). The isobutanol scouts visited 110, 1 and 1 designs above 15.8 % (up to 22.9 %) but returned designs at 1.7 % and two with no IRR. The ethanol scouts never exceeded 15.4 % and returned 8.1–12.7 %.

**(d, e)** Titers and metabolic proteome of each returned design. Pdc and the ALS→Aro10 enzymes (ALS, Ilv5, Ilv3, Aro10) commit carbon to ethanol and to isobutanol. The light shades are the terminal alcohol dehydrogenases (Adh1, Adh6), which carry flux only when their upstream branch is expressed: e.g. Adh6 in the ethanol-titer design and Adh1 in the isobutanol-yield design are idle. Translation (0.110 g·(g DCW)⁻¹) and housekeeping (0.245) sectors are the same in every design and are omitted. Dotted line: the penalty-free budget (0.135). Designs beyond it grow more slowly (growth factors ×0.84, ×0.63, ×0.30).

**(f)** Share of each campaign's trials making isobutanol (logit scale) vs the IRR of its best visit (open) and of its returned design (filled). ◇: the 1,000 seeds (27.9 % making isobutanol; best 22.9 %).

In these campaigns neither objective alone returned a profitable co-production design. The TRY objectives explored the isobutanol region but returned low-IRR designs. The unseeded profitability objective stayed at an ethanol-only plateau. Seeded with the scouts' trials, profitability search reached the highest IRR found by any of the eight campaigns.
