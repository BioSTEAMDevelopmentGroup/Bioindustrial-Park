**Figure S2 | Design fingerprints of the returned designs, and of the isobutanol scouts' best visits.**

* **Rows.** The starting strain (scenario A); the design each campaign returned (argmax of its own objective; filled markers); and, in italics with open markers, the highest-IRR trial ("best visit") of each isobutanol TRY scout. Trial numbers are simulated indices.
* **Columns.** Native capacities are fold change vs the starting strain (Pdc k<sub>3</sub> 5.81, Adh1 k<sub>6</sub> 2.82 and Adh6 k<sub>17</sub> 0.1077 g·L<sup>−1</sup>·h<sup>−1</sup>; glycolysis multiplier 1); the colour is clipped at 1/16× and 16×. The isobutanol-pathway capacities (ALS k<sub>13</sub> and the Ilv5/Ilv3/Aro10 group, "Ilv5–Aro10") are absolute, in g·L<sup>−1</sup>·h<sup>−1</sup>. Inhibition multipliers scale the ethanol, isobutanol and acetate product-inhibition coefficients (search range 0.75–1.5; 1 = starting strain). Feeding variables are shaded within their search ranges (threshold and target Δ in g·L<sup>−1</sup>; spikes = actual / cap, shaded by the actual count). Outlined cells sit at a search bound.
* **Right.** IRR ("loss" = negative or no IRR; bold = above the uninformed campaign's plateau, 15.8 %); the campaign's own objective; the metabolic proteome Φ<sub>M</sub> [g·(g DCW)<sup>−1</sup>]; and the growth factor (growth is derated above Φ<sub>M</sub> = 0.1347, the penalty-free budget). The starting strain's IRR (12.6 %) is under the model version the campaigns ran with.
* **Returned strains differ in products and proteome.** The profitable TRY-informed co-production design throttles the entry to the ethanol branch (Pdc 0.33×, Adh1 2.6×) at baseline-like glycolysis (0.98×), puts ALS and Adh6 at their upper bounds, and stays within the proteome budget (Φ<sub>M</sub> 0.130, growth ×1.00). The uninformed design puts Pdc at its 4.0× bound and switches the isobutanol pathway off (ALS and Ilv5/Ilv3/Aro10 at their floors); its Φ<sub>M</sub> (0.153) exceeds the budget (growth ×0.84).
* **Returned vs best visit.** The isobutanol-yield scout's returned design (trial 842) and its best visit (trial 843) are consecutive trials. The 7.6 % higher yield it was rewarded for (0.381 vs 0.354 g·g<sup>−1</sup>) cost 21 IRR points (1.68 % vs 22.90 %): TCI 316 vs 183 MM$, batch 102 vs 31 h, growth ×0.30 vs ×0.65. The isobutanol titer and productivity scouts returned designs with Adh1 knocked down (≤ 0.002× the starting strain) that make ≤ 0.2 g·L<sup>−1</sup> ethanol and have no IRR; their best visits keep Adh1 at 1.4× and make 4.0 and 50.1 g·L<sup>−1</sup> ethanol alongside 34.8 and 29.9 g·L<sup>−1</sup> isobutanol.
* The starting strain is shown for reference: its zero isobutanol-pathway capacities and its target Δ (4.1 g·L<sup>−1</sup>) lie below the search floors (0.001 g·L<sup>−1</sup>·h<sup>−1</sup> and 5 g·L<sup>−1</sup>), so its cells carry no outline.
